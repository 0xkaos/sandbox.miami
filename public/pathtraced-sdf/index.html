Process / 01

Nothing here is modeled.

The sculpture and distant skyline are described entirely as mathematics in a fragment shader. Two periodic fields are rotated through one another and constrained by a soft volume; seeded towers, domes, and spires rise behind it. Each pixel traces a new light path every frame, becoming quieter and more precise as samples accumulate.
