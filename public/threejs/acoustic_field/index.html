About this view

These views share a frozen, full-precision pressure-energy field from the chamber. Contours intersect it with planes. Strands follow an artistic tangent direction while staying on an energy surface. Membranes join equal-energy points; mist fills bands around those same levels.

Smoothing changes the preview only. The original energy, pressure, mask, and chamber settings remain in the saved snapshot. This captures a moment, not a resumable simulation. More particles do not increase the field's resolution.

Curve and surface extraction stops at the sampled chamber boundary. Cutaways expose the inside without adding caps.
