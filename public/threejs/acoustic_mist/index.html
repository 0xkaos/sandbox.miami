About this model

The chamber's three-dimensional wave equation runs continuously in a worker. Mist follows bands of time-averaged squared pressure directly, without simulating particles.

This is a live visualization with sound propagation slowed for visibility. It is silent and does not simulate audio at playback speed. The three colors describe neighboring energy levels, not permanent differences in air density.

Walls can return 0–100% of pressure amplitude. Gentle loss in the medium remains at 100%; the open outlet lets energy escape. Brightness follows field strength relative to its peak since restart, so bursts and reduced amplitude can fade.
