About this model

A 3D wave equation carries pressure through the chamber, reflects it from lossy walls, and lets it spread through the outlet into an absorbing exterior region. Blue is rarefaction; amber is compression.

Particles drift toward minima of time-averaged pressure energy. These are quiet pressure nodes, not permanently low-density air. Their motion is exaggerated so patterns develop in seconds.

This is a qualitative acoustic study, not a calibrated loudspeaker design tool. It omits gravity, particle collisions, elastic walls, and the full acoustic radiation force. Dimensions and frequencies are physical; pressure and particle response use relative units.

The waves run in slow motion. Burst mode makes the first arrival, reflections, and decay easier to see. Particle redistribution is useful when comparing configurations.
