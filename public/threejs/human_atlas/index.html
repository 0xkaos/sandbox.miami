THE RECORD BEHIND THE MAP

Evidence, with its edges showing.

A global population record with a deliberately denser set of stories in Europe and the Mediterranean. Each layer has its own limits.

Population

Actual HYDE grids, summed to 1°, 0.5°, or 0.25°. Charts and exports use the 1° reference grid. Linear interpolation between dates. Before 10,000 BCE, headcounts are unavailable. Missing data never means zero people.

Changing territories

Global colors crossfade between snapshots. Reviewed Near East and European areas use dated intervals. Named peoples are approximate source areas, not surveyed frontiers. Modern coastlines remain fixed.

Ancestry & movement

Routes are schematic. Study-specific colors blend at selected receiving locations; intermediate fractions and footprints are illustrative. Colors do not represent race, language, or nationality.

Population scale

The same density always produces the same height at the chosen scale and magnification. No year-by-year normalization. The default logarithmic mapping compresses large differences.

Archaeological evidence

Corded Ware and Bell Beaker burial dots use modelled date intervals; Levant survey dots use broad archaeological phases. EUROEVOL associations have no calendar date in the selected table, so they remain searchable but off the timeline. Pleiades dots show broad gazetteer period associations before 1700 CE, not continuous occupation or a count of excavated sites. Modern name and location associations remain searchable but stay off the dated map.

Languages

Selected dated EDH inscriptions attest Latin or Ancient Greek at findspots. Glottolog 5.3 language points appear only at the 2017 endpoint as a modern reference. Neither source supplies prehistoric language territories.

Heritage sites

Reviewed historical phases appear at their dates; inscription years stay separate. Century-wide dates may describe uncertainty, not continuous occupation. Search the full UNESCO catalog for source descriptions and undated sites.
